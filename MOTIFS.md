# Decorative motifs — where each one is used

All motifs are original vector drawings (`images/dongson-*.svg`), made for this site in the
spirit of the ancient **Đông Sơn bronze-drum** art of Vietnam (c. 700 BC – 100 AD).
They are *not* traced from, and do not reuse, any modern artwork or photo.

| Motif | File | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Sun star (ngôi sao mặt trời) | `dongson-sun.svg` | Ornament under every page title and section heading (site-wide) |
| Drum face with ring of Lạc birds | `dongson-drum.svg` | Faint, slowly turning watermark, bottom-right of every page |
| Flock of 3 Lạc birds | `dongson-flock.svg` | Home hero, drifting across the top |
| Lạc bird frieze (birds, zigzag, dotted circles) | `dongson-frieze-gold.svg` | Gold band along the bottom of the footer (every page) |
| Long-beaked bird (chim mỏ dài) | `dongson-longbeak.svg` | Blog page heading, right side |
| Crested bird (chim mào cong) | `dongson-crested.svg` | Projects page heading, left side |
| Round-headed bird (chim đầu tròn) | `dongson-roundhead.svg` | Contact page heading, right side (the "messenger") |
| Spotted deer (hươu đốm) | `dongson-deer.svg` | About page, foot of the portrait column |
| Single Lạc bird | `dongson-bird.svg` | Not placed (spare, for logos or print) |

Placement and strength live at the bottom of `css/style.css`
(section "WHERE EACH DONG SON MOTIF LIVES") and in `css/header-footer.css` (footer).
Change `opacity` there to make a motif fainter or stronger.
A credit line about the inspiration is shown in the footer in EN / VI / JA (`footer.motif`).

## Also in this version
