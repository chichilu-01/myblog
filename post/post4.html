Why I love cooking

My hobby is cooking. On my days off I often try new recipes at home, or cook meals for friends and family.

I think cooking helps build creativity and concentration. Above all, seeing the happy faces of the people eating is my greatest joy.

I especially enjoy making Japanese and Western dishes, and I feel a real sense of accomplishment when they turn out delicious.
