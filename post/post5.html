The Pizza My Girlfriend Made

2026-07-08 Hoang Minh Phuong Cooking

A heartwarming homemade pizza night

Today my girlfriend made pizza for the first time. She got creative with the toppings, and the finished pizza looked and tasted like something served in a restaurant.

The moment I took a bite, the flavor of the cheese spread through my mouth, and the crust was wonderfully crisp. Above all, knowing she made it thinking of me was the best seasoning of all.

Delicious food enriches the heart.

Time spent sharing good food is truly precious in daily life — I felt that again. Next time, I'd like to think up the toppings together and try making a new pizza.
